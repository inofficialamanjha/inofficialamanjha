LinkedIn Banner Prompt

LinkedIn profile cover image · AI image-generation prompt

Banner size

Upload size: 1584 × 396 pixels (recommended by LinkedIn for personal profile cover images) · 4:1 aspect ratio

File: JPG or PNG, under 8 MB

Optional working master: 3168 × 792 pixels (2×, same 4:1 ratio) for editing; export and upload the final file at 1584 × 396.

Do not use the LinkedIn Pages (company) cover size for a personal profile — those are separate specifications.

Wide panoramic LinkedIn profile cover banner, 4:1 landscape aspect ratio, final size 1584 × 396 pixels. A calm, premium, editorial abstract illustration for a backend and platform software engineer who works on distributed systems and agentic AI. Composition: a restrained network of fine, thin lines and small softly lit nodes, concentrated in the right third and upper area of the frame. Small clusters of nodes are linked by orderly, gently curving paths, and a few flow lines converge toward one calm coordinating node, suggesting reliable services working together and quiet orchestration. Keep the left side, and especially the bottom-left area, quiet and low in detail. Leave a wide, clean, uncluttered band of negative space across the center for optional text added later. Keep every meaningful element well away from the outer edges so cropping on different screens does not cut anything important. Palette: deep navy to dark slate-blue background with a subtle matte texture; soft cool grey and muted steel-blue linework; a single restrained warm amber accent on two or three nodes only. Style: minimal, geometric, softly dimensional vector illustration with fine linework, gentle diffused lighting and subtle depth. Clear contrast between lines and background but low visual noise overall — sophisticated, mature and understated, like the cover of a technical book or an architecture journal. Sharp, high resolution, clean edges. Do not include any text, letters, numbers, code, logos, brand marks, watermarks, people, faces, robots, brains, server racks, circuit-board patterns, holograms, neon sci-fi effects, lens flares or busy rainbow gradients.

Generate, resize and upload

  1. Paste the prompt into your image tool. Choose a 4:1 (or the widest available landscape) ratio at the highest available resolution — many tools cannot output exactly 1584 × 396.
  2. Crop to 4:1 first, then resize to 1584 × 396 with the aspect ratio locked (no stretching or distortion).
  3. Add the optional text overlay, then export as JPG or PNG under 8 MB.
  4. On LinkedIn: Me → View Profile → camera icon on the introduction section → upload, reposition if needed → Apply.
  5. Preview on desktop and mobile. Your profile photo sits over the cover image, so check that nothing important is hidden behind it or cut off at the edges.

Layout guidance (not an official LinkedIn safe area): keep the lower-left quiet because the profile photo overlaps the cover image there on current layouts, keep key details away from all edges, and put any text in the center/right. LinkedIn notes the image's appearance changes with browser window size and screen resolution, and does not publish an exact pixel safe zone for profile covers.

Use only original artwork. Avoid employer logos, internal project names, metrics, or confidential work in the banner.
